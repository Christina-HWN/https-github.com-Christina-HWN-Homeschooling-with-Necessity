Homestead Homeschool Toolkit

Free, practical tools for turning real-world homestead work into intentional, documented learning.

Start here: These resources work together. Use the mapping guide to identify the academic learning, the unit plans to build structured studies, and the logs to document what the student actually does and learns.

Build Your Homestead Learning System

1. Map it Identify the academic subjects, questions, skills, and outcomes inside a project.
2. Plan it Use a unit study to turn the project into a sequence of intentional learning.
3. Log it Record dates, activities, time, academic connections, location, and evidence.
4. Save it Keep photos, worksheets, graphs, notes, plans, measurements, and reflections in a portfolio.
