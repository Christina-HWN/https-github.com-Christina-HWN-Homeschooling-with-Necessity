Project Ideas

🌱 Garden Math

Measure beds, calculate spacing, track germination, graph growth, and compare harvest totals.

🔬 Investigation Notebook

Choose a question, make a prediction, record observations, collect evidence, and explain what changed.

🏠 Homestead Systems Map

Map water, food, energy, waste, shelter, and transportation systems and look for connections between them.

💰 Real-World Economics

Compare prices, plan a small project budget, track costs, calculate savings, and evaluate trade-offs.

📚 Primary Source Detective

Study an original photograph, document, map, object, or recording and separate observation from interpretation.

🛠️ Build & Improve

Design a useful object or system, test it, record what failed, revise the design, and explain the improvement.

What's next for HWN: This library is designed to grow. Future additions can include printable packs, project guides, subject-specific resource lists, family-friendly skill challenges, accessibility supports, and original HWN investigations—while keeping the collection organized instead of overwhelming.
